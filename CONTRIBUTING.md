## What We're Looking For

DigitalOddities collects **digital curiosities**—strange, absurd, or fascinating artifacts from the digital world. We're looking for:

- Broken or glitched digital elements
- Surreal system messages or notifications
- Absurd digital rituals or superstitions
- Creative ASCII art or emoji compositions
- Bizarre tables or rankings
- Digital urban legends or folklore
- Museum-style catalog entries for digital artifacts

## How to Contribute

### 1. Choose a Theme

We rotate through 10 themes, one per week. Each theme has a specific focus:

| Theme | Focus | Example Content |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| `#404 Relics` | Broken web elements, cache debris, expired interfaces | Dead links, broken buttons, cached fragments |
| `#Pixel Anomalies` | Visual glitches, compression artifacts, distorted icons | Melted avatars, glitched sprites, pixel errors |
| `#Cache Oracles` | System messages, error prompts, device notifications | Prophetic error messages, time-bending alerts |
| `#Table Hauntings` | Absurd tables, weird rankings, fake administrative forms | Lost tab rankings, ghost attendance sheets |
| `#ASCII Summoning` | ASCII art, text patterns, terminal sigils | WiFi sigils, keyboard landscapes, bracket labyrinths |
| `#Emoji Folklore` | Emoji rituals, digital customs, platform traditions | Low-battery crowns, office emoji seasons |
| `#Future Collection` | Museum-style artifacts, catalog entries | Whispering USB drives, self-copying tickets |
| `#Algorithm Superstitions` | Traffic rituals, posting superstitions | 3:03 AM posting, nine-time refresh rituals |
| `#Distorted Notices` | Absurd announcements, fake official statements | Cloud traffic notices, meme migration alerts |
| `#Cloud Urban Legends` | Digital ghost stories, forum myths | Sleeping tabs, incognito mode mirrors |

### 2. Create Your Artifact

Follow this structure for your artifact:

```markdown
# #ThemeName #ArtifactTitle

Artifact ID: DO-THEME-001  
Status: Description of condition  
Location/Source: Where it was found or created

Your creative description of the artifact goes here. Explain what it is, how it was found, and why it's interesting.

[Visual element: ASCII, emoji, table, etc.]

> Interactive Prompt: Invite others to contribute or expand on your artifact
```

### 3. Submit Your Contribution

Choose one of these methods:

#### Option A: Open an Issue

1. Go to the [Issues](https://github.com/yourusername/DigitalOddities/issues) tab
2. Click "New Issue"
3. Select the appropriate theme template
4. Fill in the form with your artifact details
5. Click "Submit new issue"

#### Option B: Submit a Pull Request

1. Fork the repository
2. Create a new file in the appropriate theme directory under `exhibits/`
3. Add your artifact content
4. Commit your changes with a descriptive message
5. Open a Pull Request with a title that includes the theme and artifact name

#### Option C: Share in Discussions

1. Go to the [Discussions](https://github.com/yourusername/DigitalOddities/discussions) tab
2. Click "New discussion"
3. Select the "Artifact Showcase" category
4. Share your artifact idea for feedback before formal submission

## Artifact Structure

All artifacts should include:

1. **Title**: A descriptive name for your artifact, including the theme tag
2. **Artifact ID**: A unique identifier (e.g., `DO-404-001` for 404 Relics)
3. **Status**: Description of the artifact's condition (e.g., "Semi-transparent", "Glitchy", "Whispering")
4. **Location/Source**: Where the artifact was found or created
5. **Description**: A creative narrative explaining the artifact
6. **Visual Element**: ASCII art, emoji, table, or other Markdown-compatible visual
7. **Interactive Prompt**: A call to action for other contributors

## Content Guidelines

- **Keep it respectful**: No personal attacks, harassment, or inappropriate content
- **Be original**: Submit your own creations or properly attributed adaptations
- **Stay Markdown-only**: No external images or resources
- **Keep it light**: Focus on the strange and absurd, not the harmful or offensive
- **Follow the theme**: Ensure your artifact fits the theme you're submitting to

## Style Tips

- **Be creative with formatting**: Use Markdown features like code blocks, tables, and quotes
- **Add personality**: Give your artifact a unique voice and backstory
- **Make it shareable**: Create content that's easy to copy and share
- **Invite interaction**: Encourage others to build on your artifact

## Review Process

1. **Initial review**: Maintainers will check your submission for compliance with guidelines
2. **Feedback**: You may receive suggestions for improvements
3. **Approval**: Once approved, your artifact will be added to the collection
4. **Showcase**: Featured artifacts may be highlighted in weekly showcases

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I submit multiple artifacts?

Yes! We encourage regular contributions across different themes.

### Do I need to be a programmer to contribute?

No! All you need is basic Markdown knowledge. No coding skills required.

### How often are themes rotated?

Themes rotate weekly, with a 10-week cycle.

### Can I suggest new themes?

Yes! Open a Discussion or Issue to suggest new theme ideas.

### What happens if my artifact is not approved?

You'll receive feedback on how to improve your submission. Feel free to revise and resubmit.
